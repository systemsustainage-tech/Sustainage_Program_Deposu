## Privacy Policy (Sustainage SaaS)

This document describes how Sustainage (“we”, “our”, “us”) collects, uses and protects personal data processed through the Sustainage sustainability reporting platform (“Service”).

### 1. Data Controller
- The data controller is the subscribing customer organization.
- Sustainage acts as a data processor on behalf of the customer for most processing activities.

### 2. Categories of Data
- Account data (name, email, role, authentication details).
- Organization data (company name, sector, country, identifiers).
- Sustainability data (environmental, social, governance indicators, uploaded reports and documents).
- Technical data (log files, IP addresses, browser information, usage statistics).

### 3. Purposes of Processing
- Providing and operating the Service.
- Ensuring security, fraud prevention and abuse detection.
- Improving functionality, performance and user experience.
- Complying with legal obligations.

### 4. Legal Bases
- Performance of contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) for core Service functionality.
- Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) for security, abuse prevention and analytics with appropriate safeguards.
- Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) where explicitly required (e.g. certain marketing communications).

### 5. Data Retention
- Account data is retained for the duration of the contract and a limited period thereafter for legal and accounting purposes.
- Sustainability reports and underlying data are retained according to the customer’s configuration and legal obligations.
- Logs are kept for a limited time for security and troubleshooting, then anonymized or deleted.

### 6. Data Sharing and Transfers
- Data may be shared with sub‑processors (e.g. hosting providers, email delivery services) under written data processing agreements.
- International transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses), where applicable.

### 7. Security Measures
- Encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest where supported.
- Role‑based access control and tenant isolation by company.
- Monitoring, rate‑limiting and abuse detection for API access.

### 8. Data Subject Rights
End users may exercise rights under applicable data protection law (access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection) via their organization (data controller). Sustainage supports the customer in fulfilling such requests.

### 9. Contact
For privacy questions, data subject requests or security concerns, contact the customer’s data protection contact or the Sustainage support team as specified in the main contract.
