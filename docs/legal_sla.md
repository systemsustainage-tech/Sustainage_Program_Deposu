## Service Level Agreement (Sustainage SaaS)

This Service Level Agreement (“SLA”) describes the service levels applicable to the Sustainage sustainability reporting platform (“Service”).

### 1. Scope
- This SLA applies to production use of the Service by subscribed customers.
- It does not apply to beta, trial, sandbox or test environments.

### 2. Availability
- Target monthly uptime: **99.5%**.
- Scheduled maintenance windows will be announced in advance and excluded from uptime calculations.
- Unplanned downtime is measured based on monitoring data and incident records.

### 3. Support
- Standard support hours: business days, local working hours of the support region.
- Support channels: email and/or ticketing system as defined in the main contract.
- Initial response time targets:
  - Critical (P1 – service unavailable or major data loss): within 2 business hours.
  - High (P2 – major functionality impaired): within 4 business hours.
  - Normal (P3 – non‑critical issues, questions): within 1 business day.

### 4. Incident Management
- Incidents are tracked from detection to resolution with unique IDs.
- For critical and high‑severity incidents, customers receive:
  - Acknowledgement and status updates.
  - A short post‑incident summary with cause and mitigation steps.

### 5. Backups and Recovery
- Regular backups of production data are performed and stored securely.
- Recovery time objectives (RTO) and recovery point objectives (RPO) are defined in the main contract or data processing agreement.

### 6. Customer Obligations
- Maintain up‑to‑date contact details for technical and administrative contacts.
- Use supported browsers and follow security best practices (e.g. strong passwords, 2FA).
- Inform Sustainage promptly of suspected security incidents or misuse.

### 7. Exclusions
The SLA does not cover:
- Issues caused by customer infrastructure, networks or third‑party dependencies outside Sustainage control.
- Misuse of the Service, unauthorized changes or failure to follow documented guidelines.

### 8. Changes
Sustainage may update this SLA from time to time. Material changes will be communicated to customers in advance where feasible.
