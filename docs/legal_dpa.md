## Data Processing Agreement (Sustainage SaaS)

This Data Processing Agreement (“DPA”) forms part of the main subscription agreement between the customer (“Controller”) and Sustainage (“Processor”) and governs the processing of personal data by Sustainage on behalf of the customer.

### 1. Subject Matter and Duration
- Sustainage processes personal data solely for the purpose of providing the Service.
- Processing continues for the duration of the main agreement and any legally required retention period.

### 2. Nature and Purpose of Processing
- Hosting, storage and management of sustainability‑related data.
- User authentication and authorization.
- Logging, monitoring and security controls.
- Analytics for performance and service improvement.

### 3. Types of Personal Data and Data Subjects
- Types of personal data:
  - Identification data (name, email, role).
  - Organization data (employer, department).
  - Technical data (log entries, IP address).
- Data subjects:
  - Customer employees and contractors using the Service.
  - Other individuals whose data is entered into the platform by the customer.

### 4. Obligations of the Processor (Sustainage)
- Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller.
- Ensure confidentiality, including appropriate confidentiality obligations for personnel.
- Implement technical and organizational security measures as described in the main agreement and security documentation.
- Assist the Controller in fulfilling data subject requests where technically feasible.
- Assist with data protection impact assessments where relevant.
- Notify the Controller without undue delay after becoming aware of a personal data breach.

### 5. Sub‑processors
- Sustainage may engage sub‑processors for hosting, email delivery, monitoring and similar services.
- A list of current sub‑processors is made available to the Controller on request.
- Sustainage ensures that each sub‑processor is bound by written data processing terms equivalent to this DPA.

### 6. International Data Transfers
- Where personal data is transferred outside the customer’s jurisdiction, Sustainage ensures appropriate safeguards (e.g. standard contractual clauses or equivalent mechanisms) are in place.

### 7. Audits
- On reasonable prior notice, the Controller may request information or third‑party audit reports demonstrating compliance with this DPA.
- Where legally required and technically feasible, the Controller may conduct or commission audits subject to reasonable limitations.

### 8. Return and Deletion of Data
- Upon termination of the main agreement, and at the Controller’s choice, Sustainage will:
  - Return personal data in a commonly used format, and/or
  - Delete or anonymize personal data, unless retention is required by law.

### 9. Liability
- Liability is governed by the main agreement. This DPA does not extend or increase the parties’ agreed liability caps or exclusions.
